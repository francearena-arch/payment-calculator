# Payment Calculator audit — 31 October 2026

Scope: branch codex/stateless-calculator-netlify, version 5.3.1 audited; 5.3.2 changes export wording only. Source review of calculation, context validation, PDF/JSON, Netlify email handler and headers; independent numerical tests; browser regression. This is a code/model audit, not certification, external penetration test, pricing approval or proof of production delivery.

## Decision

NOT approved as a complete tariff-accurate merchant cost calculator. The implemented simplified annual model is mathematically consistent for valid, finite inputs. Linked reference documents are NOT a tariff engine. Published fees, conditions, caps and transaction fixed components do not automatically feed into calculations. Real quotes require validated inputs and Pricing approval.

## Actual formulas

For each payment category: allocated volume = annual volume × turnover share / 100. Transactions default to annual transactions × turnover share / 100; optional actual counts override this.

- Split Blend cards: allocated volume × entered card rate / 100 + allocated transactions × entered fixed fee. No IC or Scheme Fee added again.
- IC++ cards: allocated volume × (entered ASF + entered assumed Interchange + entered assumed Scheme Fee) / 100 + allocated transactions × entered fixed fee.
- TWINT: allocated volume × separate TWINT rate / 100 + allocated transactions × entered fixed fee, in both models. No card IC or Scheme Fee.
- Minimum top-up: max(0, monthly minimum × 12 − card acquiring − interchange − scheme). Includes card fixed fees in acquiring. Excludes TWINT, terminals, surcharges and other charges. This contract interpretation requires confirmation.
- Surcharge: entire annual volume × entered surcharge / 100 (includes TWINT).
- Rent: monthly per-terminal rent × entered terminal count × 12. Positive rent with blank/zero count blocks calculation.
- Other: monthly fees × 12 + annual fee + full setup cost in the first year.
- Annual total sums these components. Savings = competitor total − Nexi total; display absolute difference, classify equality below CHF 0.005.
- Scenario section samples eight volumes, holds average ticket and mix constant, scales transaction counts; it does not calculate exact break-even points.

## Findings by priority

### High — linked price lists do not participate automatically

Only entered ASF/card rates, assumptions and manually entered extras are calculated. No tariff data ingestion, versioned rate table or document-derived surcharge rules exist. The 0.45% demo ASF is an example explicitly chosen by the owner, not a verified published contractual rate.

The linked Nexi price list (May 2026) contains conditional regional, commercial-card, token, authorization, B2B and other fees. None are automatically applied. The general surcharge field is applied to all turnover and cannot accurately represent conditions affecting only a subset without an externally calculated weighted adjustment. Avoid double counting with inclusive offers.

### High — IC and Scheme Fee defaults are examples, not market-valid universal tariffs

Both offers use the same global category IC/Scheme assumptions, although their actual pass-through costs may differ. No region, card-present/CNP, issuer product, wallet/token, MCC, currency, cap or fee-tier distribution is captured. Credit Visa and Mastercard are pooled; other card schemes are pooled.

Nexi's May 2026 Scheme Fee sheet has both percentage and fixed EUR/transaction components. The calculator has percentage assumptions and a generic CHF fixed-fee field per payment category, but does not automatically convert or add scheme-specific EUR components. Manual fixed fees are classified in acquiring, not in the scheme breakdown. Recalibrating a percentage at one ticket size does not preserve accuracy when ticket size changes.

Official COMCO releases distinguish Mastercard domestic debit card-present pricing (0.12% with conditions/cap) and Visa domestic debit pricing (average ceiling 0.15%, category-specific rates/cap). Existing 0.20% defaults cannot be described as universally current domestic rates. Do not replace them indiscriminately with a single regulatory number: applicability matters.

### High — minimum per transaction is not modelled

The linked price list lists at least CHF0.20 per transaction for the service package. There is no per-transaction minimum-rate rule in the code. The monthly minimum field is not a substitute. A CHF5 ticket at 1.2% produces CHF0.06 rather than CHF0.20 if that minimum applies. Actual basket distribution, not merely average ticket, is needed to calculate transaction-level caps/floors exactly.

### High — turnover input can silently change decimal entries

Reproduced: entering `500000.50` in annual turnover becomes `50’000’050`, because the input formatter discards every non-digit. Negative signs and pasted mixed text are also discarded. Current code expects whole-unit digit entry without making that restriction safe. Fix parsing/validation before use with real customer data. This audit release does not change business input semantics.

### Medium — annual minimum is not an exact monthly minimum

The implementation applies max(annual fees, 12 × monthly minimum). Summing actual monthly minima differs for seasonal volume distributions. Confirm contract basis and capture monthly distribution or explicitly label the annual approximation.

### Medium — inconsistent transaction and turnover mixes accepted

Reproduced actual-count mode accepts 1000 transactions in a category with 0% turnover, provided total transactions match. This can produce fixed costs on zero turnover and hide missing volume-based IC fees. Validate or explicitly warn about incompatible mixes; define zero-value transaction handling.

### Medium — defaults, precision and output limitations

- Demo remains flagged after subsequent manual changes; this is conservative but does not indicate which fields were changed.
- No realistic upper bounds or finite-result guard. Extremely large finite inputs can overflow arithmetic. Numerical validation needs safe limits and finite checks.
- Floating-point arithmetic is rounded for presentation only; no invoice rounding per transaction/month. The model cannot promise invoice reconciliation.
- PDF uses standard Helvetica: unsupported Unicode characters become `?`; JSON retains original text. It exports totals, input assumptions, fixed fees and breakdowns but not the same visual result layout as the web page.
- Optional blank costs become zero. This is permitted by current design but means missing charges may look like free services.
- Calculation visibility checks channel/pricing; PDF/JSON additionally validate customer, purpose and competitor. Browser readiness alone is not an all-fields validation certificate.

### Security and operations — partially verified

User strings in the result markup are escaped; generated PDF uses text rather than HTML execution. No browser storage/database is used by the current app. CSP restricts scripts/connect destinations and denies framing. Secrets and recipients are server configuration.

Email tests cover fixed recipient, original PDF byte preservation, stable idempotency key, origin rejection, bad PDF/hash, missing config, method, and provider failure. Browser flow mocks mail; no actual production email delivery or receiving mailbox was verified. Origin headers can be spoofed outside browsers, so they are not authentication. The configured platform rate limit must be confirmed in Netlify; fixed-recipient PDF injection/spam is a remaining public-endpoint risk. PDF magic-byte/hash checks do not establish trusted calculator-generated content. API acceptance is not inbox delivery; no durable queue, delivery webhook or guaranteed retry exists. No external penetration test or full WCAG conformance test performed. Responsive views tested in Chromium; actual iOS Safari not certified.

## Source interpretation

- Sources section is explanatory text plus links, not executable tariff configuration.
- IC/Scheme input values do enter the formula for IC++ card volumes; never for Split Blend or TWINT.
- PDF and JSON record the assumptions used. They do not prove these assumptions match external documents.
- No separately uploaded markup price list was identified by Library title searches for Preisliste/Markup (uploaded files). Findings above use the specific public price lists linked by the application. A separate internal markup schedule must be supplied or identified for a line-by-line review.

## Recommended next work

1. Correct unsafe input parsing, reject non-finite totals and incompatible transaction mixes.
2. Obtain Pricing-approved assumptions and a definitive markup/fee schedule with applicability and effective dates.
3. Decide between a transparent indicative calculator with manually approved effective rates, or a detailed tariff engine with regions, products, transaction minima/caps, fixed scheme charges and CHF conversion. Do not silently add every published surcharge.
4. Agree minimum-fee basis, costs included in Split Blend, treatment of TWINT and optional extras; then implement regression cases from actual statements.
5. Confirm live mail delivery, platform controls and pilot acceptance before rollout.
